# Stay@Link Brochure Project - Complete Deliverables

## Project Overview
Professional A3 landscape brochure design for Stay@Link PMS system targeting business hotels and accommodation facilities across Japan.

## Design Concept
- **Theme**: "宿泊業務を、つなげて速く" (Connect and accelerate hotel operations)
- **Aesthetic**: Blue-based color scheme with modern, advanced digital feel
- **Tone**: Professional, trustworthy, and technologically advanced
- **Target**: Business hotels and accommodation facilities nationwide

### 1. Design Files

- **Back Side HTML**: `staylink_brochure_back.html` - Complete back side design

## Design Features

### Front Side (Hero/Overview)
- **Hero Section**: Stay@Link logo with catchphrase
- **Benefits Grid**: 4 key benefits in 2×2 layout
  - 予約・チェックインの省力化 (Reservation/Check-in efficiency)
  - リアルタイム情報共有 (Real-time information sharing)
  - 集計・分析の可視化 (Analytics visualization)
  - 周辺機器・システム連携 (System integration)
- **UI Showcase**: Dashboard mockups showing key metrics
- **CTA Section**: Contact and demo request button

### Back Side (Detailed Information)
- **Feature Overview**: 4 main system features
- **Workflow Diagram**: Step-by-step hotel operations process
- **Integration Grid**: Compatible systems and devices
- **Benefits Section**: ROI metrics and advantages
- **Company Information**: Contact details and support info

## Color Scheme
- **Primary Blue**: #1E3A8A (Deep blue for trust and reliability)
- **Secondary Blue**: #3B82F6 (Bright blue for digital elements)
- **Accent Blue**: #60A5FA (Light blue for highlights)
- **Supporting Colors**: White, light gray, medium gray

## Typography
- **Headlines**: Helvetica Bold, 24-28px
- **Body Text**: Helvetica Regular, 12-14px
- **UI Elements**: Monospace for technical specifications

## Technical Specifications
- **Format**: A3 Landscape (420mm × 297mm)
- **Bleed**: 3mm on all sides
- **Resolution**: 350 DPI for print
- **Color Space**: CMYK
- **File Formats**: .ai (Illustrator) + PDF/X-1a
